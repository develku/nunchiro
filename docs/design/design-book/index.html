Nunchiro · design book · v0.2 draft

The quiet room, built to measure

Every part a builder needs, drawn in four themes with its exact values. Shape, type, spacing, motion and state signals are fixed rules; colour comes from the theme. Concept 4, the recommended blend: 눈치 is the idea, the precision instrument decides how numbers are shown, Liquid Glass stays on the outer chrome.

Each part is drawn four times: Nunchiro light and dark (the default theme, hand-set) and Catppuccin Latte and Mocha (the two catalog themes in the bundle, coloured by the derive rule). Builders: read README.md first, copy values from here, import tokens.css; the move order is in migration.md. The hover, pressed and focus states are drawn still so you can read them.

Principles

Seven rules a screen can break

If a new screen keeps these seven, it will look like Nunchiro even where this book has no drawing.

Foundations

Colour roles

Colour is a theme. A part never names a hex value; it names a role. Three surfaces, three text steps, three tint steps, one accent for focus, and the state colours.

The -text twins exist because a colour that reads as a dot often fails as words: needs-you, failed and near-limit each have a mark colour (3:1) and a words colour (4.5:1). In dark themes they are usually the same value. Faint is never text.

Foundations

Theme derive rule

Any Ghostty theme gets the same roles from its own colours. Every role that carries meaning is stepped toward black (light theme) or white (dark theme) in 5% steps until it reads; nothing else in the theme changes, and the terminal keeps the theme's colours as written.

Why palette 3 mixed with 1: a theme names no "warm" colour. ANSI yellow pulled a third of the way to red lands on an orange in nearly every theme (Latte: amber to #da6227; Mocha: cream to peach #f7c4ad), and it stays apart from the yellow used for near-limit. Where the two still sit close (Mocha), the words carry the difference: a needs-you row says "needs you", a gauge says "88%".

Rules

Writing for the screen

Short, English, sentence case. Name what the person sees, not how it is built. A button says what it does; the result says it happened.

CaseSentence case everywhere: "Open diff", "New session". Caps only in the 10 px captions (USED, 5H).
ButtonsA verb, one to three words: Allow, Open diff, Switch account. "…" when it opens a dialog that asks more.
State wordsExactly: working, needs you, idle, done, failed. Waiting text in a header: "waiting for you".
NumbersDigits, tabular: 4:12, 2h, 12m, 61%, 122k / 200k, exit 1. No "about".
ErrorsWhat happened, then what to do: "Claude stopped: the usage limit was reached. It resets at 14:20." No "oops", no apology, no code alone.
ConfirmationsPast tense, one line, with Undo when possible: "Session closed".
Never"Please", exclamation marks, "simply", internal words (seat, lane, core, PTY) where a person reads them, two names for one thing.
New stringsEnglish only until release; the seven other languages come in one pass. Leave 30% room for German and Korean growth.

Don't
Error!Oops, something went wrong (code 128). Please try again!
Rules

Accessibility

Every theme passes the same gate. Words carry every state, so nothing depends on colour or motion alone.

Text4.5:1 for all text, meta, placeholders and captions included, on every surface it can sit on (the check below). Text over chrome glass is measured on the glass over the page.
Marks and edges3:1 for state dots, rings, the focus ring, gauge values and control edges (checkbox, radio, switch off).
Never colour aloneEach state has its word next to its dot; failed and needs you differ in shape too (still dot vs glowing dot).
FocusVisible 2 px accent ring on keyboard focus, never hidden behind a sticky bar or a card.
Hit size24 × 24 at least (WCAG 2.5.8): buttons 28 and 24, compact rows 24, icon buttons 24. Smaller marks (the 6 pip, the close x) sit inside a 24 target.
Motionprefers-reduced-motion stills the dots, the swell, fades and gauge travel.
Transparencyprefers-reduced-transparency turns every glass surface solid.
Increase contrastprefers-contrast: more draws a 1 px --faint edge on fields, cards and glass panels.
Screen readersIcon buttons have a name; a needs-you change is announced once in a polite live region ("Fix login test needs you"); dots are aria-hidden and the word is read.
