Nunchiro polish directions one screen, five looks · draft for a decision, 11-21

Same screen everywhere: tree (Assistants + one project, three sessions, one waiting for you), a chat with three tool rows and an answer, the permission card above the composer, the right rail, the status bar. Fixed in all five: no outlined chips, buttons or cards; one accent per view; a small type scale on an 8 px grid; rail icons without labels (the name shows as a tooltip, drawn once on Decisions).

A · Quiet native T3 Code-like restraint

Lines out, surface steps in. No borders anywhere: the sidebar, the selected row, the tab, the card and the composer are told apart by one step of tint and by spacing.

One accent. Orange means "your turn" and nothing else (the dot on the waiting row and tab, the Allow button). Working is a grey spinner; blue and the black buttons are gone from this view.

Three type sizes (11 / 13 / 15) and two weights, everything on an 8 px grid; rows 32, tab strip 36, status bar 24.

Rails without labels (tooltips on hover), translucent macOS sidebar, shadow only on floating layers (here: the tooltip).

A+B · Glass layers A plus Liquid Glass on floating layers only

Everything in A stays flat. Glass is kept for the three things that float: the permission card, popovers (the usage popover is open here) and the command palette.

Real blur over content: the chat scrolls under the permission card (the diff block shows through it); the usage popover blurs the tree and the chat edge behind it.

Why it helps: a floating layer no longer needs a border or a dark fill to stand apart, so the screen keeps one visual weight. The dark shot shows the command palette open.

A+B+C · State light A+B plus agent state as quiet light

State is light, not chips. The rail bars, dots, spinners and badges go. A working session carries a thin light line that travels along its tree row and its tab.

Waiting for you is a warm glow at the row's edge, under its tab and on the permission card's edge, breathing slowly. The words stay ("needs you", "4m"), so state never depends on colour alone.

The usage bar glows only near a limit (Codex 5h 88% here); under that it is plain grey.

Calm by rule: two lights only (cool for working, warm for you), slow timing, and with reduced motion every light holds still (tick "Hold motion still" above).

D · Soft depth Arc / Raycast-like cards on a desk · light only

Separate cards on a tinted canvas. The tree, the chat and the widget rail are rounded panels with gentle shadows; the icon rail, tabs and status bar sit on the canvas itself.

No lines, depth instead: the open tab is a small raised pill joined to the chat card; the permission card floats one level above the chat.

Trade-off: the friendliest of the five, but the gaps cost about 16 px each way and the shadows need care in dark themes and the 400+ imported themes.

E · Editorial iA Writer / Linear-docs-like · light only

Built for long reading. Warm paper background, a narrower column, 16 px body at 1.75 line height; a serif (Iowan Old Style) for the session title and the answer's headings.

Monochrome UI with one ink accent (deep blue-black): your message is a quoted line with an ink rule, the Allow button and the waiting dots are ink. No orange.

Trade-off: the calmest for reading answers, the weakest at showing "this one needs you" at a glance across many sessions.
